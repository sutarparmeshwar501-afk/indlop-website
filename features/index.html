02 Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don’t.

A social network that gets out of the way. INDlop is being developed as a place to share, listen, and stay in the loop without making life feel like content.

The good parts, kept

Made for the people
you actually know.

Every part of INDlop starts with a simple question: does this help people feel closer, or does it just keep them looking?

01 / CIRCLES

Small circles,
big feeling.

The product direction puts personal relationships and the people you choose at the center of sharing.

02 / FEED

A feed that
breathes.

A calmer, more considered feed is part of the product direction. Specific feed behavior has not been confirmed here.

03 / WORDS

Words over
performance.

INDlop is intended to make room for sharing and conversation, including creator-led work.

04 / CONTROLS

Plain-language choices.

Clear, understandable choices are an important design goal. The final controls will depend on the product as it is built.

05 / PORTABLE

Keep what you make.

Respect for people’s work and relationships is part of the product direction. No data-export or portability feature is being claimed here.

06 / ATTENTION

No attention games.

Privacy and a considered user experience are design priorities. Google AdSense is loaded on this public website; see the Privacy page for the current disclosure.

INDlop is built around a different default for social connection.
