What is INDlop, exactly?
INDlop is a social platform in development, intended for people, communities, and creators. The final product features and availability will be described as they are confirmed.
How is INDlop different from other social apps?
The website describes a product direction, not a complete list of live app capabilities. Google AdSense is loaded on this public website. Details about the app’s features, monetization, and data practices should be confirmed before launch.
Who can see what I post?
The product direction emphasizes user choice about sharing. Exact audiences, defaults, and editing options have not been confirmed on this site.
Will INDlop be free?
Pricing and membership details have not been announced on this site.
When can I join?
This site does not confirm an opening date or operate a connected waitlist yet. The form is currently a local demo and does not register you.
