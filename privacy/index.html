04 Privacy

Your life is not
our business.

Private by default is more than a setting. It is the foundation. Your data stays yours, your circles stay yours, and your attention never gets auctioned.

Privacy, without the footnotes

Principles you can
actually understand.

This page describes current website behavior and broad product principles. It is not a substitute for a reviewed, launch-ready privacy notice.

✓
Private by default

The product direction is to give people clear choices about sharing and audience. Exact controls are not confirmed here.

✓
Privacy-first principles

Privacy is a stated product principle; final data practices should be confirmed in a launch-ready policy before the platform opens.

✓
Portable by design

Respect for user content and choice informs the product direction. No export or portability capability is being claimed here.

✓
Human controls

Clear, plain-language controls are a design goal; the specific controls are not represented as live functionality.

What this means in practice

Privacy should feel like a quiet room, not a puzzle.

The product direction prioritizes understandable privacy choices. The platform is still in development, so these pages do not describe a live set of account controls.

Introduction Welcome to INDlop ("INDlop", "we", "our", or "us"). INDlop is a digital platform designed to help people connect, share content, communicate, and participate in online communities. This Privacy Policy explains how INDlop may collect, use, store, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, use our services, create an account, or interact with our platform. By using INDlop, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent, we will obtain it separately. 2. Information We May Collect Depending on the features you use, INDlop may collect the following categories of information. 2.1 Account Information When you register for an account, we may collect your name, username, email address, phone number if provided, profile picture, date of birth where required, and authentication information. 2.2 Profile Information You may choose to provide a biography, interests, profile details, links, and other information you wish to share. Information displayed on a public profile may be visible to other users and, depending on the settings, to visitors who do not have an account. 2.3 Content You Share We may process posts, captions, comments, photos, videos, messages, and other content you submit through INDlop. The visibility of content depends on the features and privacy settings available to you. 2.4 Communications If you contact our support team, submit a complaint, or send feedback, we may collect your contact details and the contents of your communication. 2.5 Technical and Usage Information Where applicable, we may collect IP addresses, browser type, device information, operating system, log data, approximate location derived from technical information, and interactions with our services. This information may be used for security, troubleshooting, analytics, and service improvement. 2.6 Cookies and Similar Technologies Our website may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies for essential functionality, security, preferences, analytics, and advertising where enabled. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not function correctly if essential cookies are disabled. 3. How We Use Information Depending on the services you use, we may process information to: - Create and manage user accounts. - Provide profiles, feeds, posts, comments, and messaging features. - Deliver content and remember user preferences. - Respond to support requests and complaints. - Detect spam, fraud, abuse, and security threats. - Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. - Improve platform performance, reliability, and user experience. - Measure usage and understand how our services perform. - Display and measure advertisements where advertising is enabled. - Process payments or subscriptions if such features are offered. - Comply with applicable legal obligations and respond to lawful requests. We will not describe a feature as active unless it has actually been implemented. 4. Public Content and User Visibility Information you choose to publish publicly, including profile details, posts, comments, and public interactions, may be viewed, copied, shared, or indexed by other people and search engines. Please avoid publishing sensitive personal information publicly. Private or restricted content may still be processed by INDlop's systems and authorized service providers as necessary to provide the service, maintain security, or comply with the law. Privacy settings and content visibility may vary by feature. 5. Messages and Private Communications If private messaging is available, INDlop will process the information necessary to deliver and maintain those communications. Access controls and security measures will depend on the implemented messaging system. Do not assume that messages are end-to-end encrypted unless INDlop explicitly confirms that feature is active. We may process relevant information to investigate reports of abuse, address security incidents, and comply with applicable law. 6. How We Share Information INDlop does not sell personal information as a business practice. We may share or permit access to information in the following circumstances, where applicable: Service providers: Hosting, cloud storage, authentication, email delivery, analytics, security, customer support, and payment providers may process information on our behalf. Other users: Information you publish or choose to share may be visible to other users according to its visibility settings. Advertising partners: If advertising services are enabled, advertising partners may receive or collect certain technical information through cookies or similar technologies. Legal obligations: We may disclose information where required by applicable law, a valid legal process, or a lawful request from a competent authority. Safety and security: Information may be processed when reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users, enforce our policies, or defend legal rights. Business changes: Information may be transferred as part of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or transfer of business, subject to applicable legal requirements. We do not authorize service providers to use personal information for purposes that are inconsistent with their contractual obligations or applicable law. 7. Advertising and Google AdSense If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, advertisements may be displayed on INDlop. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses, and other information as permitted by their policies and applicable law to serve ads, limit repeated advertisements, measure performance, detect invalid activity, and personalize advertisements where permitted. Users may review or manage Google's advertising preferences through: https://adssettings.google.com/ Information about Google's advertising technologies is available at: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads The use of advertising cookies and personal data will be subject to applicable consent and disclosure requirements. The availability and personalization of advertisements may depend on the user's location, browser settings, consent choices, and the advertising services actually enabled on INDlop. 8. Analytics and Tracking If analytics services are enabled, INDlop or its service providers may collect information about website visits, page interactions, performance, and usage patterns. This information may be used to understand how users interact with the platform and improve our services. We will update this policy to identify relevant services where necessary. Analytics and non-essential tracking will be implemented in accordance with applicable consent requirements. 9. Data Storage and International Transfers Information may be stored or processed in the countries where INDlop or its service providers operate. Where information is transferred internationally, we will apply the safeguards required by applicable law. We take reasonable steps to ensure that service providers protect information appropriately. 10. Data Retention We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, maintaining security, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may vary according to the type of information and the reason it was collected. Information may be retained for a longer period where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. 11. Account Deletion and Data Removal Where account registration is available, users may request account deletion through the available account settings or by contacting INDlop. We will handle deletion requests in accordance with applicable law and our operational requirements. Some information may remain in backups for a limited period or be retained where legally required, necessary for security, or needed to resolve disputes. Deleting an account may not immediately remove content already copied, shared, or independently stored by other users. 12. Data Security INDlop takes reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, and misuse. No website, application, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their login credentials and report suspected account compromise or security incidents promptly. 13. Your Privacy Rights Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to: - Request access to personal information about you. - Request correction of inaccurate information. - Request deletion of personal information. - Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. - Raise concerns or complaints about data processing. - Exercise other applicable privacy rights. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request. We will respond within the time limits required by applicable law. 14. Children's Privacy INDlop's intended audience and minimum age requirements will be described in its applicable Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. Where parental consent or additional safeguards are legally required, we will apply the relevant requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information unlawfully, contact us so we can review the matter. 15. Third-Party Websites and Services INDlop may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party integrations. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. INDlop is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites. We encourage users to review the relevant policies before sharing information with third parties. 16. Legal Compliance and Law Enforcement Requests INDlop may process or disclose information where necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect the rights and safety of users, investigate suspected unlawful activity, or enforce its terms and policies. We will assess requests according to applicable legal requirements. 17. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy as our services, technologies, or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date. Where required by law, we will provide notice or obtain consent for material changes. 18. Contact and Privacy Requests For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns, contact INDlop through its official contact page: https://indlop.com/contact Please include sufficient information to help us understand your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Additional contact details will be published here when officially designated for privacy matters. 19. Important Notice This Privacy Policy describes categories of information and processing that may apply depending on the features and third-party services actually used by INDlop. It must be kept accurate and updated as the platform evolves. It does not replace applicable legal notices, consent mechanisms, Terms of Service, or other compliance obligations.
