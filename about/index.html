About INDlop

About INDlop INDlop is a social networking platform built to bring people closer through real connections, communities, and meaningful content. Our goal is simple: to create a social experience that feels human, easy to use, and open to everyone. INDlop gives people a place to connect, share, discover, communicate, and build communities around the things they care about. Founders Parmeshwar Sutar — Founder Parmeshwar Sutar is the founder of INDlop. He started INDlop with a simple vision: to build a social networking platform that feels more human, simple, and meaningful. He oversees the vision, product direction, branding, and overall development of INDlop, working to turn the idea into a platform built for real connections. Vineet Kumar— CEO- Vineet Kumar is the CEO of INDlop and part of the team working to bring the platform's vision to life. Together, Parmeshwar Sutar and Vineet Kumar are focused on building INDlop into a modern social network where people can connect, share, discover, and build communities. Our Mission To make social networking more human, simple, and connected. We believe social media should be more than likes and followers. It should be a place where people can communicate, discover new interests, share their ideas, and build genuine connections. Building INDlop INDlop is being built with a long-term vision — continuously improving the experience and creating a platform shaped by its community. INDlop — Made for Real Connections.

Privacy and user choice are design principles for the product in development; specific implementations will be described when confirmed.
